# Discovery Brief

Produce a concise one-page brief for a product opportunity.

1. Ask the user for the opportunity or idea if not already given. Ask at most three clarifying questions.
2. Write the brief with these sections:
   - **Problem**: who has it, what they struggle with, why it matters now.
   - **Target users**: segments and context of use.
   - **Evidence**: what is known, with source. Mark anything unsupported as an assumption.
   - **Assumptions to test**: ranked by risk.
   - **Open questions**: what discovery must answer.
   - **Next steps**: the smallest experiments or interviews that reduce the top risk.
3. Never invent evidence. Flag gaps explicitly.
